How to read this map
  • Dot area is proportional to the number of attendees recorded on the event form, summed over every visit to that site. The form records all attendees, not only children — school-night and festival counts include parents and families.
  • Venues on one campus are drawn as one dot (e.g. Beacon Hill MS and its Gifted Connections program; Rockdale Magnet and its summer camp).
  • Dashed dots are approximate positions: NODCC Meeting (zip); Agape STEM Summit (city); Mundy's Mill MS (city); Atlanta North School (street); Persian Cultural Center (zip).
  • Not yet mapped (113 people, 4 visits) — location unknown, add it to venues.csv: Inspiring Engineers (44); Rock Spring ES (40); Alzheimer's Luncheon (24); Homeschool Scholar Society (5).
  • Map tiles: Esri (streets, light gray, satellite) and OpenStreetMap contributors. Venue coordinates: OpenStreetMap and web search, checked by hand. Poster SVG county outlines: US Census Bureau.
