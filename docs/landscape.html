How to read this
  • The height of the horizon is the number of people reached in that academic year (Aug–Jul); the ground line is zero. The ridge is a smooth curve through the yearly values that never rises above or dips below a real value.
  • Years from the NeuroEngage event form are computed live from the same data as the dashboard; earlier years come from history.csv. A year listed there is never added to the form's count.
