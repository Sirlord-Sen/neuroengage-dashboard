Volunteer engagement & repeat service
Volunteers by number of events served
  • Outreach is running at 3.3 events/month — ahead of the proposal's Goal 1b target of doubling from one to two events per month.
  • Crews average 4.8 volunteers per event (proposal baseline: 3–4), and 37 of 98 volunteers (38%) have served at two or more events — every name is captured on the intake form, so repeat service is trackable today.
  • 14 different people have led at least one event.
  • The signature lesson plans named in the proposal (Touch-a-Brain, Build-a-Brain, Build-a-Neuron) ran at 32 of 40 events.

Method notes
  • This report is the “breadth” arm of the NeuroEngage evaluation plan — the event database tracking trainees and learners engaged. Depth measures (self-efficacy surveys, focus groups) are collected separately.
  • Charts measure the grant reporting window only. NeuroEngage outreach has run for years before this fellowship (see the program’s annual DMAP infographics); the dashed baseline paces summarize that prior performance level.
  • Target paces on the charts come from the funded proposal: Goal 1b doubles outreach from one to two events/month; the program's stated baseline is at least 1,000 K-12 students & members of the public and ~100 volunteers annually.
  • Source: Microsoft Forms outreach reports (outreach_export_2026-05-25.csv, outreach_export_2026-08-12.csv), merged so a later export can correct but never erase an earlier value.
  • 3 earlier pilot events (before Aug 2025: 104 attendees, 9 hours) precede the reporting window and are not included here; they remain on the live dashboard.
  • Academic terms: Fall = Aug–Dec, Spring = Jan–Apr, Summer = May–Jul.
  • Attendance entered as a range is counted at its midpoint and flagged as an estimate; “event hours” is the duration of each event, not person-hours.
  • Volunteer counts are floors: group entries (e.g. “lab members”) and unnamed helpers are not counted; spelling variants of the same person are merged.
